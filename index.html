    The rules
    • Slow scalper: only when the 1-hour trend is not falling. When the 15-min RSI is 30 or lower, a demo limit buy 0.3% below the price, filled only if real trades go below it. Sell at +2%, or −1% stop-loss, or after 72 hours.
    • Trend following: buy when the hourly close is above EMA20, EMA20 is above EMA50 and the close breaks the last hour's high. Stop-loss 2×ATR that only moves up. Sell when the trend breaks.
    • Limits for each: at most half its capital per trade, a stop-loss on every trade, stop for the day after losing 3%.
    • Kill switch: BTC down 3% in 15 minutes or a 5× volume spike: close everything and pause 2 hours.
    • Fees are included (0.35% limit orders, 0.6% instant orders). A bot only follows rules faster; it does not make a bad strategy good. This is a test, not a promise.
